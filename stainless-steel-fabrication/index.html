Common questions

Stainless Steel Fabrication FAQ

What stainless steel products can you fabricate?

We fabricate railings, counters, tables, frames, brackets, supports, shelves, equipment components, and other made-to-fit stainless pieces.

Do you provide stainless steel TIG welding?

Yes. TIG welding is available for stainless projects that require controlled heat, clean joints, and a precise finished appearance.

Can you repair existing stainless steel?

Yes. We can assess cracked welds, damaged frames, railings, mounts, and components to determine the right repair.

Do you work from drawings or field measurements?

We can work from plans, reference dimensions, existing components, or an on-site review depending on the project.

Which areas do you serve?

Service areas across Broward County include Davie, Plantation, Oakland Park, Wilton Manors, Hollywood, and nearby communities.
