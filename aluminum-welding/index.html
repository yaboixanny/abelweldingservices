Common questions

Aluminum Welding FAQ

Can cracked aluminum be welded?

Many cracked aluminum parts can be repaired after proper cleaning, preparation, and assessment of the surrounding material.

Do you offer TIG and MIG aluminum welding?

Yes. The welding process depends on material thickness, access, appearance, and the component’s intended use.

What aluminum items can you repair?

Common projects include railings, frames, trailers, brackets, ramps, supports, equipment parts, and custom components.

Can you fabricate a replacement aluminum part?

Yes. We can review an existing part, dimensions, plans, or site conditions to fabricate a suitable replacement.

Which areas do you serve?

Service areas across Broward County include Davie, Plantation, Oakland Park, Wilton Manors, Hollywood, and nearby communities.
